About Me

Design meets
Engineering

I'm a UI/UX designer and frontend developer with a rare ability to operate fluidly across both disciplines. I believe the gap between design and code should be invisible — and I make it so.

My work lives in the details: the timing of a hover state, the hierarchy of a typographic system, the performance of an animation at 60fps. I build things that feel as good as they look.

Currently available for select freelance projects and consulting engagements. I work best with founders, product teams, and agencies who care deeply about craft.
